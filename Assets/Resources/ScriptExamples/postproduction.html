1. Sketch

Settings (clip folder, offsets, crop, visible layers) are saved in a separate PostProduction block in the .tilt file's metadata. The camera and lens are not copied in: they are re-read from the clip's clip.json on load. Saving never touches the recorded strokes. Nothing auto-saves.

Reads the folder's clip.json (written by make_clip_manifest.py): camera, lens, frame rate and plates. Clips are listed from Open Brush's Media Library\PostProd folder. A sketch that has been saved with a clip loads it again automatically.
